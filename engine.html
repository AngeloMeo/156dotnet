Novitec are also offering a new power upgrade kit for the 2,4 JTD engines.
This kit upgrades the 2.4 JTD with 150 Bhp (110 kW) to 190 hp (140 kW)! This is
done with new Novitec electronics, intercooler and exhaust system.

.: Autodelta :.

Autodelta offers turbo and chip tuning. Read more about it here. Power gain is from 5 Bhp
for the lightest chip tuning to 80 Bhp gain for the 3.3l conversion of the 2,5 motor.
This includes a complete engine rebuild.

.: Poweralfa :.

Poweralfa offers performance packages for the 2.0 TS in three (?) phases.
The power gain in phase 1 is 12-15 Bhp and the gain in phase 4 is approx 30 Bhp over standard.

.: Squadra Tuning :.

Squadra Tuning offers chip tuning for all 156 models. Power gain is from +10 Bhp for 1.6TS
to 20 Bhp for the 2,5 V6. The prices are not bad at all! See their webpage for power graphs.
You can find more info on this direct link.

.: Z-Engineering :.

Z-Engineering offers a Supercharger kit for the 156 2,5 V6 which increases power
from 190 to 270hp. The price is US$5000 and the mounting is fully reversible and is
of course Tuv approved. You can find more info on this direct link.
Photos and dyno will be available at a later date.

.: Madeno Racing :.

Madeno Racing offers a lot of different tuning parts. For engine tuning, they offer chip tuning
with 10-30 Bhp power gain. They also offers a 2,5 to 3.0 V6 engine rebuild which gains 260+ Bhp.
English instructions: First click on the 147 picture, then on tuning, then on producten (products),
than at "Selecteer hier een type", there you choose 156 and than click on "Toon de selectie".
Thanks to Dave for this instructions! :)

.: Monza Sport :.

Monza Sport offers tuning on all Alfa twin spark models equipped with the Bosch M.E.1.55
engine management ECU. Monza Sport will re-program the existing ECU on site to give a typical
power increase of 7-10%.

.: Kerscher Motoren :.

Kerscher Motoren offers a kompressor kit to the Alfa 156 2,5 V6. It gains +50 Bhp
and +36KW more than the factory standards.

.: AH Motorsports :.

AH Motorsports offers 3.2/3.3/3.5 and 3.8 litre engine conversions for the V6 engine.

.: Alfatech UK :.

Alfatech UK offers up to 20% percent power increase to most Alfas. This is done by
downloading information to the vehicles ECU. No chips required. Website opens soon!

.: Angel Tuning UK :.

Angel Tuning UK offers chip tuning for all the engine range for Alfa 156 at
reasonable prices. Packages with break upgrade and intake filters are also available.


At this time I'm not aware of any other motor tuning kits for the Alfa 156.
