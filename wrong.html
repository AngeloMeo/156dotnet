Here is the list of what has gone wrong with my '98 model so far:

Something went wrong with the electric window switch on the passenger side.
Previous owner fixed this under warranty. Works perfectly now.

Both headlight units has been changed. They somehow got matted. I suspect either
that the previous owner washed the car with some heavy chemical stuff :) or that
the light bulb holder got loose and touched the reflector. I know this happened
with one of the headlights, but I don't know if this matted it.
Don't know how the previous owner did this but they were changed under warranty.

After accelerating heavy and breaking hard the rear left wheel started to squeak.
I suspected it to be the break as it stopped as soon as I touched the break or
the hand break.
The dealer disassembled and reassembled the breaking mechanism on that wheel and
the sound disappeared.

I have strange noises from the window on the drivers side. Sounds like the window
is loose or something, but it's not. Don't know what is making the sound and the
dealer didn't either. It's not there all the time and somehow I got used to it. :)

I have also the sounds from the front suspension and in cold weather I also
have the sound from the right side of the dash. The front suspension can be fixed by
either lubricating it a bit or changing an non-expensive part. But somehow I got used
to these sounds as well. :)

Then there is the wiper problem described here. It probably will get worse, but so far
it's not happening that frequent so I'm able to live with it.

Guess that's it, really. Today is 03.03.2002 and my car is a 10.98 model with
29.000 km on the clock. I will update this page if there is anything to add.
Hopefully it will take a long time! :)
